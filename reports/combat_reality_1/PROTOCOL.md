# COMBAT-REALITY-1: protocol before corpus scan

No training, checkpoint creation, Policy inference or control evaluation. Start from
6eee3ba0d01f63af98ece9890ec9552a5a286a56; preserve the TL1 → TL2 → numeric chain.

Hypothesis: the all-board vanilla gate, rather than absence of attacks, explains zero
numeric coverage. Observed arithmetic relations can remain available independently of
the reliability of a complete forecast. Baseline: the unchanged NCA extractor and its
owned metadata. Only the pinned real retrain train/validation split is read (32/7 games).

Offline prototype uses the pinned Standard catalog plus the existing reviewed Outcome
token supplement (including printed Reborn stats), without changing any model metadata.
It reuses Action Outcome events and unknowns, never its candidate state.
It distinguishes observed numeric relations, conditional ordinary exchange and locally
verified results within the visible forecaster contract. Unknown reactions that can alter
listed changes block verified results. Additional raw-input, active-effect and weapon
checks may only reduce certainty. Conditional values never become guaranteed kills.

Report action denominators, per-class and anonymous per-match coverage, overlapping
refusal reasons and chosen-attack successor diagnostics. Successors must be consecutive
decision numbers within one SELF turn; duplicate identities and Reborn are excluded from
endpoint comparisons. Agreement is observational evidence, not causal proof. A fixed
small feature-stratified sample is reviewed by hand against source values and texts.

GO requires meaningful real coverage of reliable local properties, credible observed
checks and explicit missingness. Conditional arithmetic alone does not justify another
fit or a numeric residual. No run in this task authorizes training. Historical controls,
raw player identities, raw states, decision IDs and checkpoints are not published.
